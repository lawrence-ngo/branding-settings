The Painted Ladies Picnic & Architecture Tour
Victorian Architecture Walk
Alamo Square Park, San Francisco, CA
Step off the tourist trail and into one of San Francisco's most iconic neighborhoods. Led by a local architectural historian, this leisurely two-hour walk takes you through Alamo Square and the surrounding streets — uncovering the stories behind the famous Painted Ladies and the city's remarkable collection of Victorian and Edwardian homes.

Along the way you'll learn how to read a building's facade, discover the neighborhood's colorful history, and enjoy a curated picnic in the park with views of the skyline. Perfect for first-time visitors and long-time residents alike. No prior knowledge needed — just comfortable shoes and a sense of curiosity.
